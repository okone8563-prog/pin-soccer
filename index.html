Pin Soccer

Fast arcade soccer with proper pinhead players.

Match Settings

Controls

Player 1

A / D = move
W = jump
S = kick / charge shot

Player 2

← / → = move
↑ = jump
↓ = kick / charge shot

Hold kick to charge the power bar. Release to shoot. The ball's height relative to your pin changes the shot angle: low contact gives a flatter shot; higher contact creates more of a chip.
